About Us

A single partner for supply and delivery

Trade-Bridge Resources Ltd is a trading and project delivery company. We supply goods and services across construction, food, medical, renewable energy and logistics, and we lead large-scale projects in the same sectors from initiation to completion.

Whether the requirement is a single shipment or a multi-phase project, clients deal with one team and one point of contact, from first enquiry to final handover.

Supply

General sales of goods and services. We source from vetted suppliers, manage the order and coordinate delivery to site.

Project Delivery

We take responsibility for large-scale projects end to end: scoping, planning, procurement, execution and handover.

Project Delivery

From initiation to completion

On large-scale projects we stay accountable for the whole job, not one part of it. Every project follows the same five stages.

  1. 01

    Initiation

    Define the scope, objectives, budget and feasibility together with the client.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Specifications, schedules, resourcing and risk planning, agreed before work starts.

  3. 03

    Procurement

    Materials, equipment and contractors sourced through our supplier network.

  4. 04

    Execution

    On-site coordination, quality control and regular progress reporting.

  5. 05

    Completion

    Inspection, handover and documentation, with support after delivery.
